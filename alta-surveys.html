What ALTA Covers
  • Boundary and Title LinesBoundary research and field evidence used to depict the surveyed boundary.
  • Easements and Rights-of-WaySurvey-related easements and rights-of-way from the supplied record documents, depicted or noted as applicable.
  • Improvements and EncroachmentsSurveyed improvements and observed conditions shown relative to the boundary and applicable easements.
  • Zoning Information and Flood ZoneZoning information and flood-zone data included as required by the applicable standards and agreed scope.
  • Access and UtilitiesObserved access and utility information documented within the survey requirements and agreed optional items.

Why GCI for ALTA Surveys

An ALTA/NSPS Land Title Survey combines boundary and field information with a review of the title commitment and relevant record documents. It documents easements, rights-of-way, access, and other survey-related conditions.

We coordinate with your attorney, title company, lender, and project team to clarify survey requirements and work through survey-related questions before final delivery.

Not sure which survey you need? Tell us about your property and transaction. We'll help determine the appropriate survey and coordinate requirements with your transaction team.
