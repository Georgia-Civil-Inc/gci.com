Overview

Planning for Phased Park Development

Georgia Civil is supporting Coweta County with site planning and civil design for Phase 1 of Prowell Park.

Our work coordinates grading, drainage, utilities, and access for the initial recreation facilities with planning for future phases. We work from the park's broader planning direction to address site conditions, neighborhood considerations, and the infrastructure needed for the next stage of development.
