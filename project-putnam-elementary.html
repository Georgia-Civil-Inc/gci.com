Overview

Site Design and Survey Support for a New School

Georgia Civil provided civil engineering and construction staking for Putnam County Elementary School, serving grades 3-5 in Eatonton.

Our site design addressed layout, grading, drainage, stormwater, utilities, erosion control, and landscape plans. GCI's surveyors worked with our civil engineers, Breaux & Associates, and the contractor to coordinate field layout with the site plans. That connection supported construction questions and design clarifications as the work proceeded.
